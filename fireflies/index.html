Catch up

What to show someone who has been away for a few hours: what happened, what needs them, and what to do next.

  • Swipe a row left to mark it done or snooze it.
  • Tap a row for the suggested next step.
  • Pull the sheet up to see where it came from.
  • Tap Up next for meeting prep, or Ask a question.
