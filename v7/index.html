I’m Shater, a designer. The best way to describe myself is a “Systemic Native”. Everything I do is grounded in some sort of system, and that shows up in the work whether I’m in Figma or in the codebase.

I design and build CostGraph.ai: the design direction, the design system, and the frontend code those designs become.

On the side I ship Luotain for link and QR analytics, Yote for React inputs, and KernUI, a design system used by 1,200+ designers and developers.
