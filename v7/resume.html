About

The best way to describe myself is a “Systemic Native”. Everything I do is grounded in some sort of system, and that shows up in the work whether I’m in Figma or in the codebase. I design first, prototype in code, then ship it. Most of my work has been for developer tools, so designing for a technical audience is just the job I’ve had.
